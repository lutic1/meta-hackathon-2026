# Keyframe UX contract: PRD reconciliation

## Verdict

The contract is mostly faithful. Every FR that has a UI lands on a surface. The verbatim strings are exact: the Input requirement, the FR-1 error, the Spend cap message, the Privacy line, the four Disclosure lines, the Card samples, the Greeting template, the First-room welcome and the No-hint line. NFR-14 is carried item for item, and every contrast ratio in the DESIGN table checks out when recomputed. Lamplit warmth and the wry voice landed well in DESIGN.md.

The losses sit in three places:

1. **Demo-honesty copy.** "Earlier builds of the Room" was dropped from the banner. The banner is missing from the Build view even though the Build view hosts two Main demo beats. The UI never declares the finals mode.
2. **The "helps without telling" feel.** A Rung never says which Puzzle it is for. A Hint request that returns the Rung already on screen produces no visible or announced reply, and that is exactly what the UJ-2 climax needs to show. An unrequested nudge has no arrival cue. §7's scaffold rule for Rungs was not carried.
3. **Qualitative ideas.** "Lead with what the room knows", "lead with the work" and "introduce each wizard by what it consumes and produces" were quoted or made optional, but never turned into behavior.

There are also several contradictions inside the spines themselves: the register rule, the typography of the stage list, where the reminder sits, and the Build view header.

Counts: 5 high, 18 medium, 6 low.

## What landed (spot checks passed)

- **Surfaces.** Every §8 surface is present: the Capture, Play, Completion and Build view screens, the Hosted landing, the Disclosure banner, the Greeting and the narrow-screen message. The closure table maps every FR that has a UI.
- **Verbatim copy.** All §7 samples and FR strings match exactly. The Privacy line keeps its lowercase verbatim form and survives cut 3.
- **Demo honesty mechanics.** Disclosures cannot be dismissed, sit in the layout grid, never cover the Scene, and open the Triggers panel. There is no demo-only injection path, and the Timer and Event log are never scaled.
- **Privacy.** Typed hint text is discarded in the browser. Photos are sent only through `POST /builds`. Hosted mode sends a fresh Derived block and always shows the First-room welcome, never a Greeting.
- **NFR-14.** All six items are carried exactly, with behavior for each. The recomputed contrast ratios all match DESIGN's table, and every token that EXPERIENCE references exists in DESIGN.
- **Qualitative.** Lamplit warmth (materials, a single lamp accent, glow means "responds", nothing slides or bounces) and the wry voice (restraint, the speaker's portrait beside each line, no exclamation styling) landed in DESIGN Brand & Style. The rule that a Wrong attempt is play, not failure, landed in both spines.
- **Tier 2 and voice.** UJ-7 and FR-35 are kept as placeholders only, with no tokens reserved.
