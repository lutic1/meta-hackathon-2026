# Keyframe UX contract: voice and copy review

Luis Ticas · 2026-10-21

## Verdict

**Passes the builder's conditions. Fix six medium lines before the mocks.** All four builder conditions pass. The Privacy line is verbatim everywhere it appears, and the Disclosures are plain, literal and match PRD §7 word for word. The register rule is applied correctly: every plain message is free of jokes and portraits, and every in-character line has a speaker. Nothing here is critical or high.

The weak spots are smaller:

- **One name per thing.** The same thing gets two names in a few places: "clue" for an Evidence card, "Rung" next to "hint", "session" next to "play-through", three labels for the Build view link, and two wordings for a Merge.
- **First-time clarity.** A few lines are hard to read at first sight: "This lock" in a Card with nothing to point at, a bare-label Mask preview template, and "Welcome to your own room" in a Demo room.
- **Room in the side column.** The first Trickster Card nearly fills the 320 px Hint area at 1024 px.

**Counts:** 0 critical, 0 high, 6 medium, 16 low.

## Findings

Severity: **medium** means a player can misread the line, or the same thing has two names on screen. **Low** means polish, a small inaccuracy, or a voice that is weaker than it could be.

## Checked and kept

These lines were checked against the four criteria and need no change:

- **Architect stage lines.** "{n} photos of one room. Let me find my loupe.", "I've handed my notes to the Riddler. Brace yourself.", "Now we make sure every lock can actually be opened. Fair is fair." and "Hanging your room on the wall. Mind the frame." The Architect observes, each line is one or two short sentences, and none names a token.
- **Architect voice line.** "I notice everything. Mostly mugs." It is dry, observational and short.
- **Merge, Rejected and Shortcut flourishes, and the rejected Card's plain frame.** "That change was refused. {fixed message}" is plain. All ten fixed reason messages are one neutral sentence with no token.
- **Rung ladder samples.** Each sample says where to look, then what to notice, then how to use it, and gets longer at each level. None says "the answer is".
- **The Guide's Completion sign-offs.** They are wry and warm, are drawn from stats only, and never name an object.
- **Greeting template phrases for neutral and negative persistence, and for the null case.** None is about objects and none sounds like surveillance. The negative case ("just long enough to get your bearings") is kind, never condescending.
- **Plain Capture copy.** The Input requirement sentence, the iPhone tip, the faces, documents and belongings reminder, the FR-1 banner (verbatim) and its "Choose photos again" action, the 422 line, `needs_recapture`, the Hosted retry consequence, the Spend cap line (verbatim) with its consequence line, and the Build quota line.
- **Hosted landing copy.** The Access code empty, refused and rate-limited lines, "{n} of 20 builds left" (verbatim), and "{n} puzzles · 15 to 25 min".
- **Play screen labels and chips.** "Checking", "Not this · {n}", "Open", "Puzzle {n} · Nudge, Direction, Near-answer", "Current", "{label} · in hand", "Using {item label}", "Nothing in hand yet.", "Start the sequence" and "Stop the sequence", "{n} more in the Card log", the Merge placeholder, the Show all objects tip, the hint field placeholder and its accessible label. None claims the Guide read typed text (EXPERIENCE Privacy and Retention).
- **Error and guard copy.** Server unreachable, Session start refused, Session gone, unknown room, the Deleted Judge room notice ("This room was deleted on Tue Nov 10, as promised.", per FR-33), "Deleted Tue Nov 10" (verbatim), and the Leave room confirmation.
- **Register in DESIGN.md Brand & Style.** It matches the PRD rule and the EXPERIENCE register table. Headings are serif everywhere. The register is carried by the body face and the portrait. No plain message sits on parchment. Each wizard's attitude matches PRD §7.
- **Width.** Every in-character line is one or two sentences and reads correctly at any wrap. No line uses a visual position as its meaning ("on the right", "above"). The only line that depends on layout is the Show all objects tip ("in the top bar"), which is accurate at every supported width, and the Narrow-screen message replaces the whole app, so the tip never shows on a phone.
