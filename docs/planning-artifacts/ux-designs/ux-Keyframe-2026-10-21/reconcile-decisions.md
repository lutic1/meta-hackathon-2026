# Keyframe UX contract: decision reconciliation

## Verdict

The builder's visual decisions landed. Lamplit study is carried as three materials and one light: warm dark chrome, parchment for anything a wizard wrote, gilt for frames, and lamp as the single accent. The spines are dark only and reserve no light tokens. The portrait decision landed with a shared prompt block for `muse-image-1.0` that is generated once. The coach's concern scan (NFR-14 floor, no sound, 1440 x 900 with a narrow-screen message, mouse and keyboard, English only, no UI system) is in both spines. Every `{group.key}` token reference in either spine resolves to a DESIGN.md frontmatter entry (checked mechanically).

What did not land, or landed wrong:

1. **The key-screen mocks decision.** The builder chose to render mocks of the Play screen, Capture screen, Build view, and Completion and greeting. None exist, and nothing references them. The same decision says every other surface is built "from the spine tables alone", but several of those surfaces have no DESIGN component row.
2. **The voice decision, applied unevenly.** The wry, playful voice "across hints, greetings and cards" is styled well. The words themselves are thin: accepted Trickster Cards are flat statements, which works against the "Trickster especially has fun" decision; the Architect's stage lines are plain progress labels; and nothing carries the voice into Rungs, Clues and Lore, which are authored at Build time and phrased by the local model. EXPERIENCE also freezes the PRD §7 samples as verbatim, although §7 says the UX contract owns them.
3. **The qualitative ideas.** "Helps without telling" and "lead with what the room knows" are quoted but not turned into behavior. The register-by-typeface rule contradicts the builder's "serif headings" decision and its own placements.
4. **Smaller contradictions.** A 1.2 s looping pulse breaks the "nothing over 300 ms" motion cap. The live region drops the Validator's reason. Fonts come from a third-party CDN beside a Retention summary that promises to name every destination.

Counts: 2 high, 10 medium, 5 low.

## What landed (spot checks passed)

- **Lamplit warmth.** Three materials and one lamp; glow means "this responds"; fades only, with nothing that slides, bounces or shakes; a Wrong attempt "is play, not failure"; photos framed with gilt, a mat and a vignette, never tinted; parchment as a flat tone, not kitsch.
- **Dark only.** There are no light tokens. Contrast is computed for the dark theme, and each state has an ink form for parchment.
- **Portraits.** A shared candlelit oil style block, a subject line per wizard, generated once with `muse-image-1.0`, a placeholder per FR-23, and a gilt ring supplied by the UI.
- **Voice restraint.** No exclamation marks, no emoji, no "Oops". Each wizard owns one surface, and its portrait sits beside each line. The rejected Card's "Spoilsport" and the Greeting template carry the wry tone where they appear.
- **Concern scan.** All NFR-14 items with behavior for each; no sound; 1440 x 900 with the narrow-screen message below 1024 px; mouse and keyboard; voice and Tier 2 held out with no reserved tokens; English only, tagged.
- **Privacy and demo honesty mechanics.** The Privacy line is verbatim, always visible and survives cut 3. Typed hint text never leaves the browser. Disclosures cannot be dismissed, sit in the grid and never cover the Scene. No demo-only injection path exists. The Timer is never scaled.
- **Token integrity.** Every token reference in both spines resolves to a DESIGN frontmatter entry.
