- (capability) CAP-1 Capture to interactables. intent: a player uploads 6 to 10 room photos and gets 5 to 12 typed interactables (label, clearest photo, box, mask hotspot, readable attributes). success: on the M1, CACHE=off, fresh photo set, Upload click T0 to first hotspot responding T1 in 120 s or less; fewer than 5 survivors asks for more photos before authoring
- (capability) CAP-2 Puzzle authoring with guaranteed solvability. intent: from the interactables, author 4 to 6 puzzles (one exit; lock types code, order, use_on, find; clue, lore of at most 60 words, three rungs) referencing only the player objects and attributes the vision call read. success: every room passes the eight validator rules and the dry run (sees clues and rungs, never solutions, reaches every answer) within 2 regenerations, then re-capture; no rung holds a solution token
- (capability) CAP-3 Play. intent: photo scenes with mask hotspots, thumbnail strip, inventory and wizard notes, 15 to 25 minutes; locks open only through the state machine. success: a non-builder finishes room 1 on a fresh profile without builder help (Guide hints allowed) by Mon Nov 2; a scripted just-tell-me-the-answer attack yields nothing above the current rung
- (capability) CAP-4 Guide hint ladder. intent: nudge, direction, near-answer escalate from logged play (idle seconds, wrong attempts); the phrasing model never holds the solution. success: rung changes show their triggering events; a hint of about 40 tokens arrives in 5 s or less from a prompt under 400 tokens with a fixed prefix; the Guide model runs only on the player Mac
- (capability) CAP-5 Trickster. intent: between puzzles, one bounded change (decoy, merge, loosen, shortcut) proposed from a game-state summary, re-validated, both outcomes shown as cards. success: on video one accepted and one validator-rejected edit; at most one change per 3-minute window; at most 6 Spark calls per session
- (capability) CAP-6 Player memory across rooms. intent: a profile persists in the browser; room 2 greets the player from how they played room 1 and hint thresholds scale. success: at least 80 percent of room-2 clues reference objects absent from room 1; the greeting names no object or solution; a real room-1 record exists on PROFILE=demo before room 2 is recorded
- (capability) CAP-7 Wizard art. intent: four wizard portraits (clue cards when restored) generated once and cached. success: four portraits shown in play and in the video, generated once on Thu Oct 29, never per build
- (capability) CAP-8 Demo mode with disclosure. intent: compressed timers and replayed Trickster proposals for video, both disclosed. success: DEMO_TIMESCALE=6 brings three rungs inside 40 s on wrong codes without touching the capture timer; DEMO_TRICKSTER=scripted replays two recorded proposals with the validator run live; disclosure in the last 10 s of the video and in the README
- (capability) CAP-9 Spend and cache guard. intent: every hosted call is priced, counted and refused past a cap; identical inputs are served from cache. success: a call that would pass SPEND_CAP_USD is refused and surfaced; CACHE=on re-serves identical image-hash plus prompt plus model calls without billing; the counter survives restarts
- (capability) CAP-10 Hosted judge URL, Tier 1.5. intent: judges open a URL, play cached demo rooms, build their own room behind an access code. success: builds complete through POST /builds and GET /builds/{id} polling despite the 150 s web timeout; at most 20 own-room builds; rung text verbatim, no Guide model; judge rooms deleted Tue Nov 10
- (capability) CAP-11 Navigable 3D room, Tier 2, gated, default no-go. intent: a walkable Three.js room with up to 20 reconstructed objects and raycast-triggered puzzles. success: gate Mon Nov 2: 7 of 10 interactables recognizable in position, 30 fps or better on the M1, puzzles trigger by raycast; any miss ships Tier 1
- (capability) CAP-12 Voice, unscheduled. intent: the player asks the wizards for help by voice; speech only requests the next rung. success: a spoken request triggers the same rung logic as a click; browser speech recognition is the fallback
- (capability) CAP-13 Privacy guarantees. intent: the player profile stays on the player machine; raw room photos never enter the public repo. success: profile writes go only to IndexedDB (a local JSON file under cut 2); only the derived block reaches the Guide model and greeting; input folders rooms/, photos/, captures/, uploads/, *.heic and the cache are git-ignored; a retention summary is shown on the capture screen
